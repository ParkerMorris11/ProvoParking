                • No parking 1:00–5:00 a.m. in campus lots, except on-campus housing and BYU-owned vehicles under BYU policy.
                • Students and employees may not use visitor parking, even for a short stop.
                • Time-limited stalls follow the posted signs; the limit ends when enforcement ends for the rest of that lot.
                • Y and G permits cost $60 a semester (free in spring and summer). B, C and D cost $25 a month. A and U are free. YM and YV are in the housing contract.

                How to read the Areas page

                • Matches listed rules means the permit and time you picked fit the rules BYU publishes for that lot section. It does not mean a space is open.
                • Map colors show the lot category (student, employee, visitor and so on), not availability.
                • Downtown Provo lots come from Provo City's parking map. Their hours and limits are what the city recorded, not checked by us, so read the posted signs.
                • Posted signs win. Events, finals, holidays and construction can change the rules.

                Downtown Provo

                • Use garages for stays longer than two hours.
                • Enforcement works around the clock.
                • A car on a city street must move at least 400 feet every 72 hours.

                Provo Parking · checked 2026-11-05

                If you get a Provo citation

                • Respond within 5 business days.
                • No late fees while an appeal filed in time is pending.
                • If the appeal is denied, pay within 7 days of the decision to avoid late fees.
