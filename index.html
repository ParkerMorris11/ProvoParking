              Help

              Key BYU rules (Y permit)

              • A Y permit is valid in Y and U lots only. A, C, G, B, D, YM, YV and visitor lots can get you a ticket.
              • No parking 1:00–5:00 a.m. in any campus lot (this does not apply to on-campus housing).
              • Visitor parking is off-limits to current students, even for a short stop.

              BYU Parking Regulations · checked 2026-10-07

              Downtown Provo

              • Use garages for stays longer than two hours.
              • Enforcement works around the clock.

              Provo Parking · checked 2026-10-07

              If you get a Provo citation

              • Respond within 5 business days.
              • No late fees while an appeal filed in time is pending.
              • If the appeal is denied, pay within 7 days of the decision to avoid late fees.
