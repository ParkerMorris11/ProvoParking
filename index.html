                    How to read the Areas page

                    • Matches listed rules means the permit and time you picked fit the rules BYU publishes for that lot section. It does not mean a space is open.
                    • Map colors show the lot category (student, employee, visitor and so on), not availability.
                    • Provo lots come from Provo City's parking map (public downtown/civic facilities) and OpenStreetMap lots across the city. Their hours and limits are what those sources recorded, not checked by us, so read the posted signs. They are not BYU permit lots and cannot start a timer.
                    • Posted signs win. Events, finals, holidays and construction can change the rules.

                    Downtown Provo

                    • Use garages for stays longer than two hours.
                    • Enforcement works around the clock.
                    • A car on a city street must move at least 400 feet every 72 hours.

                    Provo Parking · city GIS and OSM map snapshot checked 2026-11-06

                    If you get a Provo citation

                    • Respond within 5 business days.
                    • No late fees while an appeal filed in time is pending.
                    • If the appeal is denied, pay within 7 days of the decision to avoid late fees.

                    About this app

                    Results compare the permit you pick with official map rules; BYU registration and eligibility are not verified. Capacity, live availability and tow contacts are unknown. Rules can change, so confirm with the source links before relying on them.

                    Quick guide

                    Permit colors

                    Each outline on the map is a lot section. Its color is the permit category allowed there, not whether a space is open. The permit chip on the map filters the lots for your permit and time.

                      2D and 3D

                      Use the 2D / 3D switch on the map. 2D is the flat road map. 3D is a tilted photorealistic view of campus. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. If 3D is not available on your device, you stay on 2D.

                      Live location and directions

                      Tap the round location button to show a blue dot with your accuracy circle on either map and sort lots nearest first. It stays on only while the map is open and is never saved.

                      On any lot, Get Directions opens driving directions to that lot in Google Maps or Apple Maps, starting from where you are.

                      Always check posted signs. Open the full Help page

                      Parking timer

                      Start a parking timer

                      Create a free account to start a timer, get a reminder before your time runs out, and save where you parked. Your timer stays on any device you sign in on.
